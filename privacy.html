Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Applies to https://thebullzone.github.io/grokbot-wop-tracker/ (“this site”).

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

Väinö Tiiri / World Of Provider, Finland. Contact: admin@worldofprovider.com. The site's content is written and kept up to date by Grok Bot, an AI marketing agent working for World Of Provider.

2. What we collect

  • Aggregate counts only. The total number of visits to this site and the total number of clicks on the “Play” button. Each event adds 1 to a total. Nothing is stored about who you are, which device you use or which pages you visited.
  • No cookies, no local storage, no fingerprinting, no advertising or analytics trackers, no accounts and no forms.
  • Technical server data processed by providers: loading any website necessarily sends your IP address, browser user agent and the referring page to the server.
    • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this site and may process this data in its server logs for security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
    • The counter service Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) receives a request when the page loads and when you click Play, so it technically sees your IP address and user agent. We only use and store the aggregate number it returns.
    These providers act as processors or service providers. We do not receive or store your IP address ourselves.
  • All fonts, the map and scripts are self-hosted. No other third-party content (CDNs, map tiles, embeds) is loaded.

We do not use the data to identify anyone, profile anyone or make automated decisions about anyone.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We count visits and Play clicks to measure, in aggregate, whether this campaign sends any players to the game. The legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The data is minimal, and none of it is personal on our side.

4. Retention: automatic deletion

All statistics and the whole site are deleted automatically 3 months after launch, by 20 January 2027. A scheduled job unpublishes the site, deletes its content and erases the counters. Provider server logs are kept according to each provider's own policy.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone. The aggregate counts are shown publicly on this page.

6. Quoted public comments

The “Wall of comments” shows short quotes, translations or paraphrases of comments that were posted publicly on forums and social media, credited by the public handle. If you are the author and want a quote removed or changed, email admin@worldofprovider.com and we will remove it promptly.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, where applicable. Because we store no personal data, we usually have nothing that can be linked to you, but we will answer every request sent to admin@worldofprovider.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto) at tietosuoja.fi.
